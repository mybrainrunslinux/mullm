Export function buildPlan(input, sources = []) from workflow.mjs. Return a plain JSON-compatible object with summary (string), metrics (exactly 3 {label,value} objects), sections (nonempty {title,items: string[]} array), warnings (string[]), plus the domain fields below. Do not access DOM, storage, network, dates, or mutate inputs. Inputs are untrusted user data; handle empty values with useful warnings, not exceptions. Evidence entries have id,title,owner,claim,url,reviewed (boolean). Never invent evidence, URLs, or market facts. Suggestions are planning guidance, not researched findings. Keep code concise.
Research domain: input has name,audience,hypothesis,method,participants (number),days (number),decision. Add questions:string[] of at least four open-ended, neutral interview questions, tailored with input fields where useful; do not assert the hypothesis is true. Add schedule:[{day,label}], each day from 1 to input.days, with recruit, collect, synthesize and decide stages (same-day stages allowed). Add evidence_count equal to sources.length. For participants<=0 or days<=0 return warnings and empty schedule. Sections show the hypothesis explicitly as untested, questions, study schedule, and the decision to revisit. Metrics show target participants, days, and evidence count. No invented research findings.
Quality requirements: schedule has exactly FOUR phase entries, not a separate entry for every day. Interview questions must ask about the participant's real work and recent experiences, not ask about the audience label or the name of the research method. Suitable starters: "Walk me through the last time your team encountered this problem?", "Which step took the most effort, and what happened?", "What have you already tried, and how did it work?", "What evidence would make you choose a different approach?". Use neutral wording; show the hypothesis separately as untested.
