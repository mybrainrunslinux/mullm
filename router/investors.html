What We Don't Claim

Honest Risks — read before investing
  • Single user dataset (10,300 queries, 1 developer) — generalization to enterprise-scale traffic unproven
  • Local models underperform frontier models on open-ended creative, long-form, and complex reasoning tasks
  • Requires local GPU (RTX 4090 or better) for 30B model tier — not serverless or zero-hardware
  • No enterprise SLA, uptime guarantee, or commercial support yet
  • Routing classifier accuracy (82.4%) means ~17.6% of routing decisions are suboptimal — this adds latency or cost on misrouted queries
