Crokinole Rules

Scoring Zones

The board has concentric rings worth: Outer ring = 5 pts, Middle ring = 10 pts, Inner ring = 15 pts, Center hole = 20 pts (disc removed). Discs knocked off the board score 0.

Gameplay

  • Each player has 12 discs per round (24 total shots).
  • Place your disc on your side of the outer edge, drag to aim and set power, release to flick.
  • Must-hit rule: If opponent discs are on the board, your shot MUST contact at least one, or your disc is removed.
  • Discs sunk in the center hole score 20 pts and are removed immediately.
  • 8 pegs guard the center hole.

Round Scoring

After all 24 discs are played, each player's remaining discs are scored by zone. The higher scorer earns the difference between the two totals. First to 100 points wins.

Pro Mode

Friction increases every few shots. Click the sponge on your side to re-wax the board and restore smooth sliding.

Controls

Click/tap your placement zone, drag to aim, release to shoot. Press H or ? for rules. Press Escape to close this dialog.
