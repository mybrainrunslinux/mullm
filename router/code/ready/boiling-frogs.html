Amphibian Conservation

The "boiling frog" experiment was debunked in the 19th century. Frogs do jump out of gradually heated water -- as long as they are not sedated or deceived.

But real frogs face a genuine slow-boiling crisis: habitat loss, climate change, pollution, and chytrid fungus have driven over 40% of amphibian species toward extinction. This is the real "boiling water" -- and unlike the myth, frogs can't just jump out.

Over 2,000 amphibian species are currently threatened. The global amphibian population has declined by an estimated 3.1% per year since 1970.
