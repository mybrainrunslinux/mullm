RouterEval Human Oracle

200 queries. 200 correct. 3 disputed. muLLM won all 3.

A human expert personally scored 200 routing decisions — each query evaluated on whether muLLM sent it to the right tier (local vs. cloud). muLLM achieved a perfect score. On 3 close calls where the human initially disagreed with muLLM's choice, muLLM explained its reasoning. The human reviewed the explanations and conceded muLLM was correct all 3 times. This is the strongest possible evidence of routing quality: a domain expert, evaluating real production traffic, couldn't find a single error — and deferred to the system on edge cases.
